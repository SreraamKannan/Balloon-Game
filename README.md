# 🎈 Balloon Game

A lightweight, arcade-style 2D game where you pilot a rising balloon through increasingly crowded skies while dodging incoming birds.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

Float upward as high as you can! Your goal is to guide the balloon safely through waves of birds flying across the screen. Three collisions pops the balloon, ending the run. The higher you climb, the faster the birds  appear.

### Features
- **Dynamic Difficulty:** Obstacle frequency pick up as your altitude increases.
- **Score Tracker:** Real-time altitude meter tracking your personal best score.
- **Responsive Controls:** Fine-tuned horizontal movement designed for keyboard inputs.
